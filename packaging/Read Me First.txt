Procul: a menu bar remote for Apple TV

1. Drag Procul into your Applications folder.

2. Open it. macOS will say it cannot check the app, because Procul is not
   notarized by Apple. Click Done.

3. Open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, scroll down to the
   message about Procul and click Open Anyway. You only do this once.

4. macOS asks whether Procul may find devices on your local network.
   Click Allow. It needs this to reach the Apple TV.

5. A remote icon appears in the menu bar. Click it, choose Pair, and type
   the code shown on the TV. A second code follows. That one adds the line
   showing what is playing, and you can skip it.

Control-Option-R opens the remote from any app.

Needs macOS 14 or later on Apple silicon.
